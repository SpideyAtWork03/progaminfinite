Balanstein

A happy-go-lucky, physics-based 2.5D arcade game. Move a cart left and right, catching falling objects in the boxes and buckets fixed on top.

Objects arrive from the left, right and middle. Poles at both corners deflect the side objects into a playfield full of moving central obstacles.

Engineering & production notes +

Gameplay & authoring

Full engineering ownership at Ajax Studios. Record-and-replay physics, live cart control, act/checkpoint orchestration, nine obstacle types and four cart structures. Eighteen custom inspectors, six editor windows and four property drawers support a content workflow of approximately 800 levels.

Architecture & physics

A fixed-step clock, compressed versioned recordings, jointed-body playback and checkpoint snapshots. Unity/C# architecture uses capability interfaces, SOLID principles, shared timeline systems, refactoring and code review.

Economy & progression

Game economies, resource sinks, progression, retention and replayability, with adaptive difficulty based on individual player performance and collision-timed object classification.

UI, art & localization

Animated UI across varied aspect ratios, Addressables, VFX and character-animation collaboration with technical artists. Localization across 28 languages, including Arabic right-to-left layouts.

Profiling & device budgets

CPU/GPU frame time, memory, garbage collection and rendering profiling against a 30–60 FPS target on devices with 3 GB RAM, using findings to guide optimization.

Player accounts & cloud persistence

Anonymous, Unity Player Accounts, Facebook and Google Play Games authentication; linked identities, session restoration and conflict handling. Versioned cloud saves use optimistic write locks, local caching, controlled batching and event-driven pending synchronization.

Leaderboards & service UI

Built and tested Local, Weekly and Global leaderboards. A shared data-source interface, fixed reusable row pool, cached row presentation and scroll-based pagination separate service access from UI.

LiveOps & monetization

Firebase analytics, Remote Config, Firestore and FCM experience, local/push notification integration and service-cost modeling. AppLovin MAX, AdMob and Unity Ads mediation; IAPs, AppsFlyer and Adjust attribution; monetization analytics and A/B testing.

Engineering process

Forty-seven documented architecture decisions, injected service strategies, immutable result models and per-operation cancellation ownership. A controlled test batched 28 changes into one cloud write on flush.

Production & validation

Real Android internal-track validation of authentication, saves, recovery and the Global leaderboard path. Studio delivery includes Android/iOS releases, internal, alpha and closed tests, automated Unity tests and CI/CD, alongside reviewed AI-assisted CLI/MCP workflows.
